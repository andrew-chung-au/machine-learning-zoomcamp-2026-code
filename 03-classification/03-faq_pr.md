**Question**
How many points do I need to get on the final project to pass, and what else is required to earn the certificate?

**Answer**
For the current Machine Learning Zoomcamp cohort, you need at least 7 points to pass the final project.

Because grading requirements can change for future cohorts, always verify the current pass mark on your course dashboard under the "Project outcomes" section. (Note: The course dashboard may not be available right at the beginning of the course).

Keep in mind that hitting this score is only part of the requirement. You must also complete all your assigned peer reviews to qualify for the certificate, no matter what score your project receives.
